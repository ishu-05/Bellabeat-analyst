Recommendations

Three directions for Bellabeat's Leaf marketing strategy.

RecommendationBasis
Segment activity messaging into two tracks Gentle activation nudges for the highly sedentary majority (60%); goal/performance tracking for the more active minority.
Lead with intensity and cardiovascular fitness, not step counts Steps–calories (r=0.59) and resting-HR (r=-0.69) findings both support marketing deeper physiological tracking as a differentiator.
Invest in closing the engagement gap 100% activity logging vs. 73% sleep vs. 42% heart rate — the opportunity is adoption of existing features, not new data.
