Recommendations

Three moves aimed at converting casual riders into members.

RecommendationBasis
Convert the Saturday rider Casual riders already ride at near-member volume on weekends. Reach them right after a weekend trip with a trial membership pitched around weekday commuting.
Lock in the season Casual ridership peaks hard in summer. Offer an annual membership at the summer rate while enthusiasm — and usage — is highest.
Reposition the ride Casual riders show no rush-hour pattern. Station and in-app messaging during commute windows can reframe Cyclistic as transportation, not just recreation.
